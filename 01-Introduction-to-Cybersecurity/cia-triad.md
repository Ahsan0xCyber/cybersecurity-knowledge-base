# CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a fundamental information security model consisting of three principles: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

## 1. Confidentiality

Confidentiality prevents sensitive information from being disclosed to unauthorized users.

**Examples:** Encryption, access control, and strong passwords.

## 2. Integrity

Integrity protects information from intentional or accidental modification, ensuring that data remains accurate and complete.

**Methods:** Hash functions and checksums.

## 3. Availability

Availability ensures that authorized users can access systems and data when needed.

**Methods:** Maintenance, repairs, updates, and backups.

## Key Takeaways

- **Confidentiality:** Prevent unauthorized disclosure.
- **Integrity:** Protect data accuracy and prevent unauthorized modification.
- **Availability:** Ensure reliable access to systems and data.
